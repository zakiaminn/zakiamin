# Design brief: Zaki (zakiamin.com)

**Summary**: Personal mark for Zaki Amin Ahmad, a student builder who ships real data and trading systems (The Repo Exchange, an options order-flow engine, DOMolition), aimed at recruiters for a Summer 2027 data / full-stack co-op.
**Audience**: Technical recruiters and hiring managers on data, fintech and quant teams. They meet the mark as a browser tab, the nav, a Slack/LinkedIn link card and the résumé header. Secondary: peers on GitHub.
**Brand adjectives**: precise, sharp, confident, builder, dry-witted. **Avoid feeling**: corporate, cute, generic "dev portfolio".
**Promise / big idea**: Real systems, not coursework, made by someone with a sense of humour about it.
**Competitive landscape**: Student and developer portfolios: initials in a circle or rounded square, `</>` and `>_` glyphs, curly braces, gradient blobs, hexagons, unmodified geometric-sans monograms. Data and fintech add blue, upward arrows, candlesticks, bar charts, globes and shields. Open territory: a mark with a real idea behind it, solid and flat, that works at 16 px.
**Mark type hypothesis**: The mark lives mostly as a favicon, nav mark and avatar, so a compact letterform or lettermark comes first, with a "ZAKI" wordmark for the lockup. Explore three different types.
**Must work on**: favicon (16/32 px), nav (about 28 px), apple-touch icon, OG card, résumé header, light and dark mode.
**Constraints**: Replaces Masthead ZA (no equity to keep). Name is "ZA" or "Zaki". Open exploration: not bound to the house kit for concepts; colour comes later.
**Success criteria**: Recognisable at 16 px; explainable in one sentence; does not look like Zendesk, Zorin, Zenhub, Zulip, Kotlin, npm or Vercel; reads as precise with a light edge.
**Decision-maker**: Zaki. **Deliverables (after approval)**: master SVGs, lockups, small-size cut, favicon/app-icon set, usage notes.
**Assumptions I made**: the logo can say "ZA" as a symbol with "ZAKI" beside it in lockups; uppercase is fine for the wordmark during exploration; Vercel-style solid triangles and green/red gain markers are off-limits.

## Equity audit of Masthead ZA

- Keeps nothing by request. Its weaknesses at small sizes: five horizontal bars plus two letters is a lot of detail at 16 px, and the idea (a newspaper nameplate) only works if you already know the site is laid out as a front page.

## Clichés to avoid

`</>`, `>_`, `{}`, blinking cursors, initials in a circle, hexagons, gradient blobs, lightning-bolt Z, sleep "zzz", upward arrows, candlesticks, bar charts, solid triangle (Vercel), halftone dots (the portrait already owns them on the page).

## Word map (abridged)

- **Z** → zigzag, last letter, a path across a page (the Z reading pattern), two levels joined by a diagonal
- **A** → apex, first, peak, caret
- **ZA** → the reverse of A–Z: sort descending, end to end, full range
- **builder / DOMolition / Wallbreak** → blocks, bricks, a break, a clean cut, two parts that fit
- **data analyst** → sort, rank, filter, columns, the top of the list
- **trading / exchange** → bid and ask (two levels), the spread, a fill, a tick
- **dry wit** → a joke that only works because his initials are Z and A

## Longlist (one line each, scored on clarity / distinction / simplicity / relevance / 16 px, out of 25)

1. **Shear**: a solid block split along a Z-shaped cut, the top half slid one step, built to come apart (23)
2. **Sorted**: Z stacked over A with a down arrow: his initials are the descending sort, so he's top of the list (22)
3. **One line**: Z and A drawn as one unbroken stroke, the pen never lifts (19)
4. Shared diagonal: the Z's diagonal doubles as the A's left leg (17, common monogram move)
5. Folded sheet: a paper strip folded twice into a Z (16, close to OpenZeppelin)
6. Crop marks: two printer's corner marks that read as a Z (16)
7. Spread: two price levels joined by a diagonal fill (15, needs explaining)
8. Z-pattern: the eye's reading path across a page with a start dot (14)
9. Ticker: ZAKI set as a four-letter stock ticker with an uptick for the A (14, solid triangle reads as Vercel)
10. Monospace wordmark: "zaki" on a terminal grid with slab i (13, generic code-font look)

**Building**: 1 Shear (letterform, negative space), 2 Sorted (stacked lettermark), 3 One line (monoline ligature). Three different forms: a solid block, a vertical stack, a continuous line.
